How Dracker works

Four steps from sticker to reunited.

  1. 1
    sell

    Create a tag

    Name a tag for anything you own — “House keys”, “Camera bag”, “Bike”. It takes seconds.

  2. 2
    qr_code_2

    Print & attach the QR

    Every tag gets a unique QR code. Print it, stick it on your item, and you are covered.

  3. 3
    travel_explore

    A finder scans it

    If your item goes missing, anyone can scan the QR with their phone — no app required.

  4. 4
    my_location

    You get pinged

    See where it was found on a map, read the finder’s note, and reach out to get it back.
